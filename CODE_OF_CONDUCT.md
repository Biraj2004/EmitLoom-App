# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in EmitLoom. This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Community standards

- Treat people with respect and welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience.
- Discuss the work and give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect participants' privacy. Get permission before sharing their personal information or private messages.
- Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, and unwanted sexual attention are unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For public abuse or harassment, use GitHub's reporting tools. If a maintainer has made a private contact channel available, you can also share the concern there. Include relevant links and a short description of the behavior. Keep personal details and private evidence out of public issues. [GitHub abuse reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Treat reports discreetly and share details only as needed to review or address the concern.
